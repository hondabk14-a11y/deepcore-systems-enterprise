# DeepCore Systems Enterprise Engineering Roles

## Purpose

Define the core engineering positions required to build, secure, verify, integrate, and operate DeepCore Systems Enterprise.

## Roles

### 1. Founding Blockchain & Cryptographic Systems Engineer
Builds the event-sourced ledger, deterministic replay engine, Bitcoin integration, cryptographic primitives, and core verification systems.

### 2. Principal Cryptography & Security Engineer
Owns PKI, hardware-bound identity, Ed25519, BLS, SHA-256, PQC integration boundaries, certificate lifecycle, authorization, HSM/KMS architecture, threat modeling, and security verification.

### 3. Principal Distributed Systems & Consensus Engineer
Builds HotStuff-style BFT consensus, quorum evidence, deterministic replication, Kafka/NATS transport, multi-region reconciliation, adversarial-node simulation, and formal consensus models.

### 4. Principal AI & Autonomous Systems Engineer
Builds the DeepCore AI Bridge, OpenAI-assisted engineering workflows, agent orchestration, evaluation systems, code review, diagnostics, and permission boundaries that keep AI separate from ledger authority and key custody.

### 5. Principal Bitcoin & Digital Asset Infrastructure Engineer
Builds Bitcoin Core RPC integration, block/transaction/mempool ingestion, independent SHA-256d and Proof-of-Work verification, Lightning/Liquid interfaces, transaction lifecycle processing, and DC02 anchoring.

### 6. Principal Site Reliability & Production Infrastructure Engineer
Builds Kubernetes, Docker, Helm, GitOps, Kafka/NATS operations, observability, SLOs, backup/restore, disaster recovery, rollback, incident response, and production-readiness evidence.

## Engineering principle

DeepCore follows a verified engineering lifecycle:

`BUILD -> TEST -> VERIFY -> SECURE -> DEPLOY -> OBSERVE -> RECOVER`

AI may assist engineering and orchestration, but authoritative ledger state, cryptographic keys, consensus finality, authorization, and production certification remain subject to explicit controls and verification.

## Status

These are internal project role definitions. They are not claims that these positions are currently externally advertised, staffed, or compensated unless separately documented with actual employment records.
